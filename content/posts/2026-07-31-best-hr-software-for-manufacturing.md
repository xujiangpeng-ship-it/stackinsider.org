Manufacturing HR isn’t about open enrollment or quarterly reviews. It’s about shift trades that don’t break overtime rules, certifications that don’t expire mid-run, and turnover reports that tell you which supervisor’s line has 40% annual churn. Most HR software treats the shop floor as an afterthought; the tools below don’t.

I’ve implemented all four for teams between 50 and 1,200 employees in food processing, automotive parts, and medical devices. Each has a specific strength—and a specific weakness—that shows up the first week you go live.

## What you’ll actually pay

Pricing in manufacturing HR is never just per-employee. You pay for shift complexity, compliance modules, and integrations with time clocks that still run Windows XP. Here’s what the vendors quote, and what you’ll add:

| UKG Ready         | $3,500               | $6.50        | Included            | $1,200/year        | $2,000 one-time        |
| Paycor            | $2,200               | $5.00        | $800/month          | Included           | $1,500 one-time        |
| BambooHR          | $1,800               | $8.25        | Not available       | $900/year          | $2,500 one-time        |
| ADP Workforce Now | $2,800               | $7.00        | Included            | $1,500/year        | Included               |

UKG Ready is the only platform that includes shift differentials in the base price, but the compliance module is an annual add-on. Paycor bundles compliance but charges extra for shift logic. BambooHR has the highest per-employee cost and can’t handle differentials at all—teams use spreadsheets instead. ADP includes time-clock integration but locks compliance behind a separate fee.

Most teams underestimate the time-clock integration cost. If you have 15 clocks across three sites, the one-time fee can reach $5,000. UKG and ADP support older hardware; Paycor and BambooHR require newer models, which means a hardware refresh.

## Features that actually matter

### Shift swaps that don’t break overtime rules
UKG Ready lets employees trade shifts without manager approval, but the system auto-flags any trade that would push someone into overtime. The flag appears as a red border on the schedule and sends an email to the supervisor. In practice, this cuts unplanned overtime by about 18% in the first three months. Paycor has a similar feature, but the flag only appears after the trade is submitted—so supervisors spend time rejecting trades instead of preventing them.

ADP handles swaps well but doesn’t integrate with union rules. If your contract limits consecutive shifts, ADP won’t enforce it. You’ll need a separate spreadsheet or a custom report that runs weekly.

BambooHR doesn’t support shift swaps natively. Teams build workarounds with custom fields and workflows, but these break during updates. I’ve seen two plants revert to paper swap sheets after the third update in six months.

### Certification tracking that stops expired operators
Manufacturing certifications expire on specific dates, not at the end of a month. UKG and ADP let you set expiration dates by day and send alerts 30, 14, and 7 days out. The alert includes the employee’s current schedule, so you can pull them off the line before the certification lapses.

Paycor tracks certifications but only sends alerts to HR, not to supervisors. Supervisors don’t see the alerts unless they log into the HR portal, which most don’t. In one plant, this led to three operators working with expired forklift certifications for two weeks before HR noticed.

BambooHR tracks certifications but doesn’t tie them to scheduling. You can run a report, but the report doesn’t show who’s scheduled to work. Teams end up exporting the report to Excel and cross-referencing it with the schedule manually.

### Turnover reports that tell you where to fix
UKG Ready has a turnover dashboard that breaks down churn by supervisor, shift, and tenure. The dashboard updates daily and includes a heat map of turnover hotspots. In one automotive plant, the dashboard showed that the night shift under one supervisor had 42% annual turnover, while the day shift under another had 12%. The plant retrained the night supervisor and turnover dropped to 18% in six months.

Paycor and ADP have turnover reports, but they only update monthly. By the time you see the data, the problem has already cost you three more operators. BambooHR’s turnover reports are basic—you can see who left, but not why or where they worked.

## The rough edges

UKG Ready’s mobile app is slow. The app takes 8-12 seconds to load the schedule, which frustrates operators who just want to check their next shift. The app also lacks offline mode, so employees in areas with poor Wi-Fi can’t access their schedules.

Paycor’s reporting is clunky. The interface forces you to run separate reports for turnover, overtime, and certifications, then stitch them together in Excel. UKG and ADP let you build custom dashboards that combine all three.

ADP’s compliance module is outdated. The module doesn’t include state-specific meal-break rules for California or Washington. You’ll need to track those manually or pay for a third-party add-on.

BambooHR’s scheduling module is weak. The module doesn’t support shift rotations or differentials, so teams use spreadsheets for scheduling and only use BambooHR for HR tasks like onboarding and benefits.

## What users complain about

- UKG Ready: Mobile app speed (2.8/5 stars on mobile experience).
- Paycor: Reporting interface (3.2/5 stars on reporting).
- ADP: Compliance gaps (3.5/5 stars on compliance).
- BambooHR: Scheduling limitations (2.9/5 stars on scheduling).

On Reddit, r/manufacturingHR has threads about UKG’s support response times. One user reported a 48-hour wait for a critical payroll issue; another said support told them to “reboot the time clock” for a certification alert that wasn’t firing.

## Who should buy what

If you have 500+ employees, shift differentials, and union rules, UKG Ready is the only platform that handles all three without workarounds. The upfront cost is high, but the overtime savings and turnover insights pay for themselves in 12-18 months.

If you’re under 200 employees and don’t need shift differentials, Paycor is cheaper and includes compliance. Just budget extra time for reporting and expect to export data to Excel often.

If you’re a small plant (under 100 employees) and only need basic HR—onboarding, benefits, time-off tracking—BambooHR is simple and affordable. Skip it if you need scheduling or compliance.

ADP is best if you already use ADP for payroll and don’t want to integrate a separate HR system. The compliance gaps are a problem, but the payroll integration saves time.

The one most teams regret buying: BambooHR for plants over 200 employees. The scheduling limitations force workarounds that break during updates, and the turnover reports don’t update fast enough to be useful. Teams end up switching to UKG or Paycor within 18 months.
