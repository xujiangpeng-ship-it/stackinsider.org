## What "free" really means in 2026

Most free plans exclude payroll. Gusto and Rippling both offer free HR basics but charge per-employee fees for payroll runs. BambooHR’s free tier is only a 7-day trial. The few that include payroll—like Payroll4Free—limit you to 25 employees and require manual tax filings.

Time-off tracking is usually free, but approval workflows often require a paid upgrade. Workday and UKG Pro include basic time-off in their free plans, but multi-level approvals cost extra. If your team needs more than a simple yes/no from one manager, budget for an upgrade.

Onboarding checklists are common, but e-signatures and document storage rarely are. Zoho People and OrangeHRM let you create onboarding tasks for free, but collecting signed offer letters or I-9s requires a paid plan.

Payroll4Free is the only one that includes payroll. You get unlimited pay runs for up to 25 employees, but you must file taxes yourself. The interface feels outdated, and there’s no mobile app. Teams that need payroll automation should expect to pay at least $30 per month for Gusto or Rippling.

Zoho People and OrangeHRM are solid for time-off and basic HR. Both include approval workflows, mobile apps, and integrations with Slack and Google Workspace. Neither handles payroll, so you’ll need a separate tool or manual spreadsheets.

Bitrix24 is more of a collaboration tool with HR bolted on. The free plan includes time-off and basic onboarding, but the interface is cluttered. It’s best for teams already using Bitrix24 for project management.

## What you’ll actually pay

Free plans cover the basics, but most teams outgrow them quickly.

Payroll4Free charges $25 per month for automated tax filings. Zoho People jumps to $1.50 per employee per month after five users. OrangeHRM starts at $4 per employee per month for document storage and e-signatures. Bitrix24’s first paid tier is $49 per month for ten users.

If you need payroll, budget at least $30 per month for Gusto or Rippling. Both include automated tax filings and integrations with accounting software. Rippling also handles IT and benefits, but costs climb fast—expect $8 per employee per month for HR plus $20 for payroll.

## Where each tool falls short

Payroll4Free’s manual tax filings are a dealbreaker for most. The interface hasn’t been updated since 2020, and customer support is email-only. Teams that need phone support should look elsewhere.

Zoho People’s free plan is limited to five employees. The mobile app is slow, and the reporting features are basic. If you need custom reports or analytics, you’ll need a paid plan.

OrangeHRM’s free plan includes 50 employees, but the onboarding features are weak. There’s no e-signature or document storage, so you’ll need to handle offer letters and I-9s manually. The mobile app is functional but not intuitive.

Bitrix24’s HR features feel like an afterthought. The interface is busy, and the HR tools are buried under project management features. It’s best for teams already using Bitrix24 for other tasks.

## Who should use free HR software

Free tools work for teams under 25 with simple needs. If you only need time-off tracking and basic onboarding, Zoho People or OrangeHRM will do. If you need payroll and can handle manual tax filings, Payroll4Free is the only free option.

Teams over 25 or with complex workflows should expect to pay. Gusto and Rippling are the best all-in-one options, but they’re not free. If you need payroll, budget at least $30 per month. If you need advanced reporting or integrations, budget $50-$100 per month.

The best free HR software in 2026 depends on your team size and needs. For payroll, Payroll4Free is the only free option. For time-off and basic HR, Zoho People and OrangeHRM are solid. For teams already using Bitrix24, it’s worth a look. Everyone else should expect to pay for what they need.
